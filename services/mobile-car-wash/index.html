The service

What to discuss before booking

Richmond Car Detailing Co lists Full body wash and Wheel washing. Confirm which of these services belong in your appointment and whether the vehicle is suitable.

  • A full-body wash at the agreed mobile appointment location.
  • Attention to ordinary dust, road film, and surface grime within the selected wash scope.
  • Wheel washing where it is included in the appointment.
  • A separate discussion of interior work, waxing, or clay treatment if those are also requested.

When this service fits

A mobile wash is a starting point for routine exterior cleaning when ordinary surface dirt is the main concern. It is different from a broader detail, a fabric-cleaning request, or a paint-repair job. Describe the condition and the parts of the vehicle that need attention so the appointment can be matched to the right work.

Before the visit

Prepare the vehicle and location

Confirm the exact parking location and permission to work there. Explain any gate, clearance, water, power, or runoff restriction before the appointment is accepted.

Make sure the vehicle can be reached at the agreed time. Report loose trim, films, coatings, damaged surfaces, or an area that should be avoided. Ask what needs to be available at the site rather than assuming all mobile washes use the same setup.

Know the limits

A routine wash does not automatically include interior detailing, waxing, clay treatment, polishing, or paint repair. Heavy bonded material and damage require a separate discussion. A wash should not be sold as permanent protection or as a guarantee that every exterior mark will disappear.

A useful first decision

Plan around access and weather

Explain when the vehicle is available and whether the parking location is exposed or covered. Weather and property access can affect the appointment, so confirm the plan directly. If timing is important, say when the vehicle needs to leave. A clear location and schedule help define the visit without relying on a fixed duration or universal weather promise.

Routine wash or a broader detail?

Start with the condition you want addressed. Ordinary dust and road film may fit a wash, while an interior spill, bonded exterior buildup, or a damaged finish raises different questions. Explain what you see rather than asking for the largest package by default. This helps keep maintenance cleaning separate from add-ons and specialist work that need their own scope.

Appointment planning

Agree on the work before it starts

Describe the vehicle, the condition, and the areas you want addressed. Confirm the appointment location, access, and any utility requirements. Ask for the included work and quote before booking, then review the agreed areas and any limitations at the end of the visit.

Questions before booking

Mobile Car Wash FAQs

Is a mobile wash the same as a full detail?

No. A mobile wash focuses on routine exterior cleaning. Interior detailing and other appearance treatments have their own scope and should be requested separately.

Does a mobile wash include the interior?

Not automatically. Interior vacuuming, steam cleaning, and seat shampooing are separate menu choices. Explain both interior and exterior needs if you want a combined appointment.

Can the wash take place at work or an apartment?

The location must allow the work and provide suitable access. Confirm property permission and any site restrictions before booking; the address alone does not establish approval.

Are wax and clay treatment included?

They are separately listed services. Ask whether either is appropriate and included in the quote rather than assuming a routine wash includes them.

What happens if the weather changes?

Confirm the appointment plan directly. Weather and the location may affect whether the work is practical, so discuss timing or a suitable setting before the visit.

Do I need to supply water or electricity?

Ask before booking and describe what is available at the location. The required utility setup is confirmed for the appointment rather than inferred from the phrase mobile wash.
